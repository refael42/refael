## Full description (up to 4000)

Open a tiny stand with one pan — and grow it into a restaurant empire!

🍟 COOK AND SERVE
Every dish cooks on its own equipment: fries in the fryer, burgers on the plancha, pad thai in the wok, pizza in the wood-fired oven, sushi at the sushi counter. Cooks plate the dish and set it on the pass, and waiters rush it to the table.

👩‍🍳 HIRE YOUR TEAM
Applicants come to the door with their CV. Pick cooks, waiters, dishwashers, hosts, bartenders, couriers and a shift manager. Every worker has their own skills, traits and mood — and a happy team works faster.

🏗️ UPGRADE AND EXPAND
Eight buildings, from the first diner to the Crown palace: each one bigger, with a new dining room, a kitchen in rooms (hot and cold lines, a dish room, a walk-in cooler and freezer, vegetables, pastry, butchery and a small manager's office) and more dishes on the menu.

🎨 DESIGN YOUR PLACE
Round tables, square ones, booths, and tables joined into long family tables. Red carpets, plants, an aquarium, a piano and a statue. A bar that grows with you: beer taps, a neon sign, dry-ice cocktails and a champagne tower.

🛵 DELIVERY
Couriers on scooters, a packing station and a delivery hub that grows and opens more at every level.

⭐ STARS AND REVIEWS
Fast service means happy guests, tips and five-star reviews (and some very funny ones). An anonymous inspector from the chef's guide drops by now and then — cook your best and earn stars.

🎡 AND MORE
A lucky wheel, a daily gift, festivals with exclusive trophies, a tourist bus, VIP guests, busy weekends, day and night, rain and sun — and new branches in new cities.
